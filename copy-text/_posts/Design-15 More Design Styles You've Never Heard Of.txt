# 正文

## 1-Neo Fruitger 新未来主义

![故事 Pin 图图片](6641bdecb6094fd85b2e3370bd7f8e97.jpg)

-   LIGHT GLOSSY GRADIENTS
-   BLUE SKIES MIXED WITH WATER AND CHROME
-   AIRY, SOFT LIGHTING EFFECTS AND TRANSPARENCY
-   SANS-SERIF TYPOGRAPHY
-   FLOATING ELEMENTS LIKE BUBBLES OR CLOUDS

---

- 浅色光泽渐变
- 蓝色天空与水和铬元素交织
- 轻盈柔和的灯光效果和透明度
- 无衬线字体 
- 气泡或云朵等漂浮元素

## 2-Dark Academia 暗黑学院风

![img](428d216eefac87468d7ed0fd5b73e08a.jpg)

-   MUTED, WARM COLORS
-   SERIF FONTS,OR ANYTHING THAT SUGGESTS CLASSICAL TYPOGRAPHY
-   VINTAGE TEXTURES LIKE PARCHMENT, TWEED, LEATHER, OR MARBLE
-   ANCIENT OR MAGICAL SYMBOLISM

---

- 柔和暖色调
- 衬线字体，或任何暗示古典字体的元素
- 复古纹理，例如羊皮纸、粗花呢、皮革或大理石
- 古老或神奇的象征意义

## 3-Light Academia 浅色学院风

![故事 Pin 图图片](bb3641d0e690bef613d0cc5736a0b7d9.jpg)

-   LIGHT AND AIRY COLOR PALETTES (BEIGE, CREAM SOFT YELLOWS)
-   ELEGANT SERIF FONTS (ANYTHING CLASSICAL OR IN CURSIVE STYLE SCRIPTS)
-   CLASSICAL IMAGERY (FLOWERS, BOOKS, HANDWRITTEN NOTES)
-   CLEAN COMPOSITIONS WITH SOFT, DREAMY FILTERS

---

- 轻盈通透的配色方案（米色、奶油色和柔和的黄色）
- 优雅的衬线字体（任何古典或草书风格的字体）
- 古典意象（花卉、书籍、手写笔记）
- 干净的构图，搭配柔和梦幻的滤镜

## 4-Wabi Sabi Style 侘寂风格

![img](e5196cb5d7a723ba03f15988a529d410.jpg)

-   EARTHY, ORGANIC COLOR PALETTES (THINK TAUPE, CLAY, AND MOSSY GREEN)
-   NATURAL TEXTURES (STONE, LINEN, WOODGRAIN)
-   MINIMALIST LAYOUT WITH IRREGULAR SHAPES AND SPACING
-   AN OVERALL SENSE OFIMPERFECTION AND SOFTNESS

---

- 朴实自然的色调（例如灰褐色、粘土色和苔绿色）
- 自然纹理（石材、亚麻布、木纹）
- 极简布局，不规则形状和间距
- 整体呈现不完美与柔和感
